Answers to common questions posed by General Counsels, Lead Independent Directors, and Audit Committee Chairs.

Our firm is retained either directly by the Board of Directors or through outside corporate legal counsel as specialized consulting experts. All interviews, workpapers, and preliminary evaluations are strictly shielded under attorney-client privilege and the work-product doctrine in compliance with Delaware and federal evidentiary rules.

Traditional management consultancies advise corporate executives on operational strategy and organizational restructuring. Veritas & Crown represents only the Board of Directors and independent committees. Our senior partners are former regulatory enforcement directors and corporate litigators who specialize exclusively in fiduciary liability, statutory disclosures, and boardroom accountability.

Activist investors typically attack perceived board complacency, misaligned compensation, or stale director tenure. We perform pre-emptive activist vulnerability audits, benchmark your governance against ISS and Glass Lewis standards, and assist the board in demonstrating active, independent oversight to institutional proxy voting committees.

We advise audit chairs on immediate triage, establishing whether management must be recused, deploying independent forensic accountants, and preparing disclosures if material misconduct is substantiated.
